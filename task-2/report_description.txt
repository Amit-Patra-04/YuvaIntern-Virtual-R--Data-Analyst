Project Report Description: Task 2 - Data Visualization and Insight Communication using R

For the second milestone of my Virtual R Data Analyst Internship with Yuva Intern, I developed an advanced suite of dynamic visualizations using R's ggplot2 ecosystem to translate complex telecommunications customer data into actionable business intelligence. Building upon the cleaned IBM Telco Customer Churn dataset (7,043 customer accounts across 21 variables), the objective was to move beyond basic descriptive charts and engineer purpose-built visual representations that clearly communicate behavioral trends, structural anomalies, and operational risk drivers to both technical analysts and executive decision-makers.

Across approximately 32 hours of design, iterative scripting, and narrative synthesis, I constructed seven distinct visualization architectures. To capture customer lifecycle dynamics, I built a continuous tenure trajectory line chart incorporating a 5-month moving average and annotated confidence intervals, exposing a severe first-year hazard zone where Month 1 churn exceeds 50% before dropping below 10% past Year 5. To diagnose pricing friction, I engineered a violin-boxplot hybrid displaying multimodal fee densities across broadband tiers, revealing that Fiber Optic plans carry a heavy price premium ($70–$110/month) that directly drives customer cancellations.

Furthermore, I developed a multivariate interaction heatmap cross-tabulating contract terms with internet mediums, identifying a critical 54.6% churn hazard among month-to-month fiber subscribers. To benchmark operational touchpoints, I utilized an ordered horizontal lollipop chart that visually highlights how manual Electronic Check payments incur an 18.7% excess churn penalty over the 26.54% portfolio baseline, while automated credit card and bank transfer methods provide substantial retention protection. Finally, I deployed faceted scatter plots with regression overlays, demographic intersection bar charts, and an executive diverging relative risk multiplier chart showing that long-term contracts reduce attrition probability by 89.3%. The accompanying Word document provides full narrative breakdowns, embedded R code, output evidence, and strategic retention recommendations.
