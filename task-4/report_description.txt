Project Report Description: Task 4 - Comprehensive Data Analysis Reporting and Presentation

For the capstone milestone of my Virtual R Data Analyst Internship with Yuva Intern, I synthesized all previous analytical workflows into an exhaustive, executive-ready final report on the IBM Telco Customer Churn portfolio (7,043 customer accounts across 21 variables). This culminating submission brings together data hygiene, advanced visual storytelling, inferential hypothesis testing, and supervised predictive modeling into a unified strategic document designed to communicate actionable intelligence to both technical peers and corporate decision-makers.

Across approximately 35 hours of focused synthesis, I documented the complete data science lifecycle. The report details our initial data quality audit, where 11 blank entries in TotalCharges were systematically diagnosed as newly onboarded accounts with zero tenure, justifying domain-grounded zero imputation over misleading mean substitutions. It integrates our multi-layered visual analytics suite, showcasing how continuous tenure trajectory curves, violin-boxplot pricing densities, and multivariate heatmaps exposed critical churn hazards—most notably among month-to-month Fiber Optic subscribers (54.6% churn rate) and manual Electronic Check payers (45.29% churn rate).

The report further details the inferential hypothesis testing suite, where two-sample Welch's t-tests, Mann-Whitney U tests, and Pearson's Chi-Square tests confirmed statistically significant group disparities in recurring monthly fees (p < 0.001) and account longevity (p < 0.001). For predictive modeling, a multivariate binary Logistic Regression architecture demonstrated strong discriminative power (cross-validated AUC = 0.8459), matching the non-linear Random Forest benchmark. Most importantly, the report highlights the operational value of classification threshold tuning via Youden's J index: lowering the decision cutoff from 0.50 to 0.26 elevated churn detection recall from 55.35% to 79.95%, reducing costly False Negatives from 167 to 75 accounts. The document concludes with four prioritized operational retention protocols, financial ROI projections, lessons learned, and an advanced modeling roadmap.
