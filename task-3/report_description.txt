Project Report Description: Task 3 - Statistical Analysis and Predictive Modeling using R

For the third milestone of my Virtual R Data Analyst Internship with Yuva Intern, I conducted an in-depth inferential statistical investigation and built a predictive classification architecture on the IBM Telco Customer Churn dataset (7,043 customer observations across 21 variables). The core objective was to move beyond descriptive exploration, rigorously test empirical hypotheses regarding customer churn drivers, and deploy cross-validated machine learning models capable of identifying high-risk accounts prior to service cancellation.

Across approximately 34 hours of focused analysis, I executed both parametric and non-parametric hypothesis tests. Two-sample Welch's t-tests and Mann-Whitney U tests confirmed that customers who churn incur statistically significantly higher recurring monthly charges (mean of $74.44 versus $61.27, t = 18.41, p < 0.001, Cohen's d = 0.470) and maintain dramatically lower tenure (17.98 months versus 37.57 months, t = 34.82, p < 0.001, Cohen's d = 0.893). Pearson's Chi-Square tests of independence established strong associations across contract types (Chi2 = 1,184.60, Cramer's V = 0.410), broadband technology, and payment channels.

For predictive modeling, I implemented a stratified 80/20 train-test split combined with 5-fold cross-validation. The primary multivariate Logistic Regression model demonstrated robust discrimination, achieving a cross-validated AUC of 0.8459, which matched the non-linear Random Forest benchmark (AUC = 0.8450). Diagnostic checks confirmed strong goodness-of-fit (McFadden's Pseudo R2 = 0.2873) while isolating high VIF collinearity between monthly charges and service add-on dummies. Crucially, I performed classification cutoff optimization using Youden's J index. Adjusting the decision threshold from the standard 0.50 down to 0.26 elevated churner detection recall from 55.35% to 79.95%, reducing costly False Negatives from 167 to 75. The accompanying Word document contains complete mathematical formulations, odds ratio tables, confusion matrices, ROC curves, and strategic deployment recommendations.
